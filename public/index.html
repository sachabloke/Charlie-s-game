MATHS ROYALE

Loot the chests. Solve the sums. Be the last one standing.

How to play
  • WASD or arrow keys to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot
  • Walk up to a chest and press E — answer the sum to open it
  • Harder sums = better loot (grey → green → blue → purple → gold)
  • 1–5 pick an item, click to use bandages and shields, R reload, Q drop
  • On a tablet or phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and shoots
