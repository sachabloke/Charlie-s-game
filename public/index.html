How to play
  • WASD to move, mouse to look around, click to shoot
  • Walk up to a chest and press E — answer the question to open it
  • Harder questions = better loot (grey → green → blue → purple → gold)
  • E also picks up guns and items from the ground (swaps if your bag is full)
  • 1–5 pick an item, click to use bandages and shields, R reload, Q drop, Space jump, hold Tab for scores
  • On a tablet or phone: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, FIRE button shoots
