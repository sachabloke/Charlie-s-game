# Where every sound came from

Every sound the game plays is a real recording from **freesound.org**, and every one is licensed **Creative Commons 0** (public domain: free to use, no credit required). Each sound's own page was opened and checked for the CC0 licence on 14 Nov 2026 before its preview file was fetched. The authors are credited here anyway, with thanks.

The files in this folder are **cut down and levelled** from those previews (single takes, trimmed, rumble removed, loudness matched, loops cross-faded) by build_sounds.py and build_sounds2.py (with build_cut.py). A file holds its takes one after another with a short silence between; the game plays one take at a time.

33 files, 3.1 MB in all, from 35 recordings. The second batch (birds, hens, the bell) was added on 14 Nov 2026 the same way, each page checked for CC0; the quieter bird recordings also had their steady background hiss reduced.

The third batch (18 Nov 2026: geese, jackdaw, swifts, starlings, pheasant, barn owl, hedgehog, cattle, pony whinny, horse snort) makes it 43 files, 4.4 MB, from 45 recordings. Sacha approved these ten; each page's CC0 licence was read on 18 Nov 2026 (director/sounds/CANDIDATES.md). They were cut by build_sounds3.py from the previews kept in director/sounds/raw/, with the cut points chosen from spectrograms. Nobody has listened to the cut files yet: see director/sounds/REPORT.md.

Fetched but not used (all CC0): "Footsteps Dirt Gravel" by PotatokingXII (/people/PotatokingXII/sounds/352870/); "Chaffinch (ziablik)" by urupin (122618: a city bird with an untypical song); "a buzzard call" by Jaturo (194678: a chatter, not a mew); "Common Buzzards" by farandjoun (828438); "enkhuizen catholic church" by odilonmarcenaro (120833). Looked at and rejected without fetching: "Church Bell Bb4" by bassimat (made by a computer, not a recording) and "TollBell" by daytripper (a very large bell).

If any of these sounds is wrong, the fix is to pick another CC0 recording and run it through build_sounds.py; the code-made sounds in src/mods/sound_synth.js remain only as a stand-in if a file fails to load.
